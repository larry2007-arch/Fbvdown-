Is It Legal to Download Your Own Facebook Videos? Nigeria Law Explained 2026

Downloading Facebook videos is legal in Nigeria when you download your OWN content. Many creators in Lagos and across Nigeria upload their skits, business ads, and family memories to Facebook, but Facebook does not provide a direct download button for your own uploads in full HD.

Under Nigerian copyright law, you own the copyright to any video you create. This means you have the right to save, back up, and reuse your own videos, Reels, and Stories. Using a tool like FBVDOWN to extract your own video is considered personal backup, not piracy.

When is it NOT legal?

  1. 1. Downloading someone else's movie, music video, or skit without permission to re-upload on your page. This violates Facebook Terms and intellectual property rights.
  2. 2. Downloading private videos from a group you are not authorized to share.
  3. 3. Using downloaded videos for commercial monetization without the creator's consent.

Facebook Video Formats Explained: HD vs SD vs 720p vs 1080p

Facebook Video Formats: What is HD, SD, 720p, 1080p? Which Should You Download?

When you extract a Facebook video with FBVDOWN, you will see options like HD Quality and Normal Quality. What do they mean? Which should you choose for your phone or for re-editing?

Facebook stores every video in 2-3 formats:

1. SD (Standard Definition) - 480p or 640x360: Small file size (5-15MB). Good if you have low data or small storage. Fast to download on 2G/3G. Quality is blurry on big screens.

2. HD - 720p (1280x720): This is the most common HD on Facebook. Clear for phones, laptops, and WhatsApp Status. File size is medium (20-60MB for a 2-min video). Recommended for most users. This is what we label "HD Quality" on FBVDOWN.

3. Full HD - 1080p (1920x1080): Only available if the original uploader uploaded in 1080p. Not all Facebook videos have this. If available, FBVDOWN will show it. Best for archiving, editing in CapCut, or re-uploading your own content to YouTube. File size is large (80-200MB).

Which should you download?

  • For WhatsApp Status / TikTok repost (your own content): Download 720p HD. It's clear and not too heavy.
  • For backup on laptop / editing: Download 1080p if available, else 720p.
  • For quick viewing offline with low data: Download Normal Quality (SD).

Why does FBVDOWN sometimes show only one quality?

If the original video was uploaded in low quality, Facebook only has SD version. We cannot create HD that doesn't exist. We extract the raw storage URL strings directly from Facebook's CDN, so you get the maximum available quality.
