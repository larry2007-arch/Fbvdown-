Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 28, 2026

At fbvdown, available at https://fbvdown.com.ng, we prioritize the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains the types of information we collect and how we utilize it.

1. Log Files & Metadata Routing

We follow a standard procedure of utilizing log files. These files log visitors when they visit web pages. All hosting service providers perform this as part of active analytics. The details collected within log files include IP addresses, browser specifications, ISP indicators, date and time stamps, referring pages, and the number of clicks. None of this data is linked to any personally identifiable information.

2. Google DoubleClick DART Cookies

Google is an integrated third-party advertiser on our platform. It utilizes cookies, specifically DART cookies, to serve ads to our website visitors based on their interactions on our site and other pages on the web. Visitors can opt out of the use of DART cookies by visiting the Google Ad and Content Network Privacy Policy at: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

3. No Data Storage Commitment

When you paste a URL and extract media, we act only as a routing bridge. We do not inspect your personal data, nor do we save, log, or maintain records of any video files or downloaded files. All operations are processed entirely in-memory and are instantly cleared once the download stream is complete.
